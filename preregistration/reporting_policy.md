# Preregistration reporting policy

The canonical reporting rules are in `docs/validation/VALIDATION_REPORTING_POLICY.md`.

This preregistration package binds every future run to full reporting of preregistered, null, adverse, failed and blocked outcomes; exact execution-manifest provenance; explicit `SENSITIVITY_ONLY` labels; separate external-constraint mappings; and the no-refit boundary. No selective split/scenario omission is permitted.
