# LSC External Veto Data Request Checklist

Status: Step 3 implementation artifact  

## 1. KATRIN

Required data:

- energy-response constraints;
- beta-spectrum systematic model;
- machine-readable uncertainty information where public;
- literature bounds relevant to energy-scale distortion.

Why it matters:

- constrains universal energy-response interpretations.

Constrained structure:

- trace response / energy-response transfer.

Public access:

- literature public; machine-readable integration not present locally.

## 2. IceCube

Required data:

- anisotropy constraints;
- Lorentz-violation or SME-related neutrino bounds;
- systematic treatment of detector anisotropy;
- machine-readable limits where public.

Why it matters:

- constrains physical/celestial tensor interpretations.

Constrained structure:

- traceless anisotropy if interpreted beyond detector-local response.

Public access:

- literature public; mapped dataset not integrated locally.

## 3. SME / Lorentz Literature

Required data:

- published coefficient bounds;
- mapping rules for any fixed tensor interpretation;
- energy and direction dependence of limits.

Why it matters:

- prevents hidden Lorentz-violation conflict.

Constrained structure:

- tensor-background interpretations.

Public access:

- public literature; mapping not implemented.

## 4. Solar-Neutrino Fits

Required data:

- solar Gallium consistency constraints;
- global solar-neutrino fit summaries;
- uncertainty treatment.

Why it matters:

- tests whether response is source-experiment-specific or universal.

Constrained structure:

- scalar/trace response.

Public access:

- literature public; machine-readable integration absent.

## 5. Standard Oscillation Constraints

Required data:

- three-flavor global fit constraints;
- sterile-neutrino benchmark literature;
- L/E and geometry inputs for source experiments.

Why it matters:

- keeps LSC within known oscillation constraints.

Constrained structure:

- any survival/transition behavior.

Public access:

- literature public; local integration incomplete.

## 6. Detector Calibration/Systematics Literature

Required data:

- gallium cross-section uncertainty;
- source activity calibration uncertainty;
- extraction efficiency literature;
- counter efficiency uncertainty;
- background modeling literature.

Why it matters:

- powers M2 and M4.

Constrained structure:

- trace response and detector/systematics mimicry.

Public access:

- partially public; not consolidated into machine-readable M4 package.
