# LSC Pre-BEST-2 Freeze Statement

This freeze statement is reconstructed from LSC 6.3.0 and related validation reports.

## Frozen before BEST-2-style fitting

- Treat LSC as an unvalidated phenomenological framework.
- Keep sterile neutrinos, normalization-only, cross-section/systematics, detector-systematics and null models as required baselines.
- Separate scalar trace response from traceless directional anisotropy.
- Freeze equations, parameter list, priors/bounds, datasets, comparison models, success criteria and failure criteria before looking at new fit outputs.
- Archive negative validation outcomes as valid outcomes.

## Outcomes that would support the LSC direction

- A frozen trace-plus-traceless detector-response template predicts zone/orientation/sidereal structure better than simpler baselines after parameter penalties.
- BEST-trained templates predict GALLEX/SAGE or future BEST-2 structure without re-tuning.
- Anisotropy remains stable after environmental and detector-systematics corrections.

## Outcomes that would weaken LSC

- The apparent effect collapses into scalar normalization or cross-section nuisance terms.
- Leave-one-out tests remain unstable.
- Each experiment requires unrelated tensor parameters with no shared rule.
- External vetoes from KATRIN, IceCube, solar-neutrino consistency or standard oscillation fits exclude the required parameter region.

## Outcomes that would force abandonment or major revision

- No stable sidereal/orientation/zone pattern is observed where the frozen model predicts one.
- The model cannot outperform null, normalization-only, cross-section/systematics, sterile, or detector-systematics baselines after penalties.
- Required parameter values imply a universal energy distortion already excluded by external data.
