# Negative Findings

All findings below mean **not located in the stated public/local search scope as of 2026-08-16**. They do not claim that an object does not exist.

## Frozen numerical LSC 6.3.0 object

Searched: every reachable LSC-Research commit and branch, exact-string and regex history, deletions/renames, reflogs, tags, stashes, unreachable objects, canonical and historical directories, notebook outputs, serialized-file inventories, Zenodo archives, and GitHub release metadata.

Not located: complete theta, ordering, lambda0/lambdaA values, exact f0_a/fA_a, active tensor values/orientation, complete units/frames, numerical config/defaults/bounds, executable evaluator initialization, or a frozen prediction table.

Limit: private author devices, deleted remote-only objects, unpublished media, and collaboration holdings were not available.

## BEST covariance and likelihood

Searched: BEST arXiv papers and TeX sources, publisher/DOI metadata, indexed HEPData, Zenodo, INSPIRE, GitHub, and retained audit material.

Recovered: a published summary covariance prescription, inner/outer aggregate results, run tables, systematic components, source activity, and geometry descriptions.

Not located: full 20-row/run statistical covariance, full systematic covariance, inner/outer cross-covariance, nuisance response vectors, or a joint likelihood workspace.

Limit: a direct HEPData API request returned HTTP 403. Public indexed search results were checked, but this is not proof of global absence.

## SAGE

Searched: 51Cr and 37Ar source papers and source archives plus retained timing/covariance audits.

Recovered: exposure/run tables, reference local times, marginal uncertainties, controls, apparatus descriptions, and an average source path.

Not located: authenticated run covariance, exact UTC offset/conversion contract, surveyed detector/source axes, or event/sub-run capture timing.

## GALLEX/GNO

Searched: the GALLEX reanalysis paper/source, source-run tables, timing and geometry references.

Recovered: source-run results, campaign date information, likelihood description, and effective geometry coefficients.

Not located: source-run covariance/likelihood workspace, exact UTC intervals, surveyed orientation, or a machine-readable source-to-target path distribution.

The published correlation between two solar-run analyses is not adjudicated as a source-run covariance.

## Nuclear cross sections

Searched: Bahcall and Elliott-Gavrin-Haxton sources plus the local cross-section registry.

Recovered: central values, marginal uncertainties, transition information, and a statement of shared/common uncertainty.

Not located: a complete numerical covariance or posterior sample jointly covering relevant 51Cr and 37Ar transition strengths. Status: MARGINAL_ONLY.

## External mappings

The KATRIN and IceCube numerical releases are present and validated. What was not located is an LSC-specific equation-level mapping that preserves their native physical and statistical meaning. The gap is theoretical, not a missing grid file.

## Geometry and T12

Published average geometry exists for BEST, SAGE, and GALLEX. Exact machine-readable production geometry was not located. No authoritative event/sub-run timing, surveyed orientation, complete active tensor, or full frame transformation was located for T12.
