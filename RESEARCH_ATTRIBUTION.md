# Research attribution and evidence boundary

## Repository identity

`luciferprosun/LSC-Research` is the canonical pre-BEST-2 freeze-stage repository
for the LSC research line. The freeze-stage authors recorded by both
`CITATION.cff` and `README.md` are LuciferSun and flAmeBorn.

Names retained inside `historical_archive/` are provenance-preserved historical
metadata. This evidence layer neither deletes them nor promotes them into the
current freeze-stage author list.

## Evidence statement

`evidence-chain/chain.jsonl` records file-level SHA-256 values and chronology
for the complete repository scope. It is evidence of integrity and custody,
not proof that every embedded statement is original or scientifically valid.

Third-party publications, data, reviews, and model outputs retain their own
authorship and rights. Hashing them does not claim ownership. The existing MIT
license is not changed by the evidence chain.
